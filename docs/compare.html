How to avoid the new 1% US transfer tax

Since January 1, 2026, a US law taxes some transfers sent abroad at 1% — but only if you pay with cash, a money order, or a cashier's check at a counter. Paying from a bank account, a US debit or credit card, or most digital wallets is exempt. Every number on this page assumes bank-account funding, so none of them include this tax — if you're paying cash, add about 1% of the amount you send. Read the IRS notice.
